## Background

As the product continued to grow, more clients started requesting a mobile version that would allow them to access important information without relying on a desktop device.

The existing product was primarily designed for desktop, with complex dashboards, multiple widgets, detailed data views, and advanced filters. This project was initiated to explore how those core capabilities could be translated into a mobile experience while remaining useful, clear, and consistent with the existing product.

##

## Problem Statement

The main challenge was not simply resizing the desktop interface, but deciding how its behavior and information hierarchy should adapt to a smaller screen.

On mobile, the limited screen size required a more focused experience. Complex information needed to be reorganized without removing the context users needed to understand their data.

## Goal & Scope

To design a clear and practical mobile experience that gives users access to essential product information while preserving the core value and familiarity of the desktop platform.

## Process & Solution

### Step 1 / Requirement Gathering
Mobile requirements were discussed with the Product Owner to define priority use cases and the features that had to stay accessible. This separated essential mobile tasks from desktop behaviors that were too complex for smaller screens.

### Step 2  / Benchmarking & Competitor Analysis
Several mobile products were reviewed to understand how they present and organize data on smaller screens. Familiar patterns were then applied to keep the experience and consistent with the existing product.

### Step 3  / Translating the Desktop Dashboard
The dashboard was reorganized into a vertical, scannable structure rather than showing many widgets at once. The most important information appear first, while detailed data stays available through further interaction.

### Step 4  / Designing Widgets and My Data
Each widget was adjusted for mobile, with titles, metrics, charts, legends, and supporting details arranged to stay clear on a smaller screen.

  src="/images/projects/mobile/mobile_detail_4.png"

For My Data page was simplified for mobile, making the information easier to scan without copying the dense desktop table layout.

### Step 5  / Simplifying Mobile Filters
Filters were moved to a separate section to keep the main content clear, while active selections remain visible through short labels.
