## Background

CMSmart is an online distribution platform designed to help grocery store owners access a wide range of household products more easily. As the brand aimed to reach and attract new users, the team needed a website that could clearly introduce the service, communicate its core value, and build trust from the first visit.

This project focused on designing a modern and informative website that presents CMSmart as a reliable digital solution for grocery owners.

## Problem Statement

New users needed a clear and trustworthy entry point to understand what CMSmart offers, how the service works, and why it is relevant for their business. Without a structured website, the brand had limited space to explain its value proposition, showcase its ecosystem, and guide users toward key actions such as learning more, contacting the team, or exploring the service.

The challenge was to design a website that could balance brand storytelling, service explanation, and user action in a simple and accessible way.

## Goal & Scope

To design a clean, modern, and informative website that introduces CMSmart to new users, communicates its core value, and encourages users to explore the service or contact the team.

## Process & Solution

### Step 1 / Research & Content Understanding
The work started by gathering the brand material from the team and studying several reference websites to see how comparable services introduce themselves and explain their product. This grounded the content decisions in proven patterns rather than guesswork, and clarified exactly what CMSmart needed to communicate to land with a first-time visitor.

### Step 2  / Define Website Structure & User Journey
With the content understood, the site structure was mapped to lead visitors from a first impression of the brand toward a deeper understanding of the product. The order of pages followed the questions a new visitor naturally asks, what is this, why does it matter, and how do I act, so the journey felt intuitive rather than arbitrary.

### Architecture Information
- Home: Introduce value proposition and key benefits
- About Us: Explain company background, vision, mission, journey, and team
- Ecosystem: Show CMSmart’s partners, collaborators, and business network
- Article: Share insights, updates, and useful content to educate users about the industry, product benefits, and business growth opportunities
- Career: Introduce available job opportunities, company culture, and hiring information for people who want to join the team
- Contact Us: provide clear access for inquiries and communication

### Step 3  / Design the Homepage as the Main Entry Point
The homepage carried the most weight as the main entry point, so it led with a clear hero, benefit highlights, service coverage, and partner logos. Each block was chosen to answer a trust question quickly, because a first-time visitor decides whether to stay within seconds, and the partner logos were placed early to build credibility before asking for any action.

### Step 4  / Build Supporting Pages for Trust & Exploration
Supporting pages were designed to make the site feel complete and credible rather than a single promotional page. The About Us page gave company context, the Ecosystem page showed the network and collaborations, and the Contact Us page lowered the effort of reaching the team, because trust in a new platform comes from evidence and easy access, not claims alone.

## Reflection

The work covered the full design and structure of the site, while development sat outside the project's scope. The natural next step would be to build and launch it, then track how new visitors move through the journey, so the structure can be validated against real behavior rather than assumption.
